STEM Innovation Academy Tournament

Team 221S, 2024

This event brought a complete ground-up redesign. Our first robot was a heavy 30 lbs, which meant the drivetrain motors would overheat and thermal throttle after just one match. We trimmed it down to a 20 lbs, added high-goal scoring, and implemented in PID and odometry for way better autonomous precision. A lot of late-night testing went into fixing intake tension issues and making our mobile goal clamp actually reliable, which paid off with the Design Award.
